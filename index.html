A careful guide to Pineal Guardian X and the science behind its claims

Pineal Guardian X is marketed as a supplement focused on the pineal gland, melatonin, sleep, and memory. This guide examines the product's public description alongside established information about pineal gland function, circadian rhythms, ingredient research, and supplement safety.

It is important to distinguish the seller's claims from proven results. The public sales page says the formula removes fluoride from the pineal gland and restores melatonin production, but the public evidence reviewed here does not establish those effects for the finished product. This book explains the difference.
